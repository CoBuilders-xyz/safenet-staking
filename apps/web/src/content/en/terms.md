## 1. Who we are

This interface is operated by **Backstage Online LLC** ("the operator"). The [Imprint](#/imprint) has the registered address and the contact details.

## 2. What the interface is

Safenet Staking is a web interface. It reads public data and prepares transactions for the Safenet staking contracts on Ethereum Mainnet. You sign and send every transaction with your own wallet.

The operator does not operate the Safenet contracts, the validators or the reward programme. The Safe Ecosystem Foundation (SEF) publishes the contracts and the reward data.

## 3. Self-custody

- The interface never holds your SAFE, your private keys or your recovery phrase.
- The operator cannot move, freeze or return your tokens.
- The operator cannot reverse a transaction that you signed.

## 4. Who may use the interface

- You must have the legal capacity to accept these terms.
- You must not use the interface from a restricted country. The interface checks the country of your connection and refuses access from the countries on its list.
- You must not use the interface with a wallet address on a sanctions list. The interface reads an on-chain sanctions oracle for the connected address.
